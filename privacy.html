Kalite Mobil Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Kalite Mobil is the mobile app of a Quality Management System used by healthcare institutions. It is used only by the institution's staff and connects only to the institution's own server. The institution using the app is the data controller.

Data collected

  • Name, username, e-mail and mobile phone (account and SMS verification)
  • Device name, model and device identifier (device pairing)
  • Form answers, notes, photos and files you enter in the app

Purpose

Running quality processes, authentication (SMS code), device pairing and notifications.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored only on the institution's own servers. It is not shared with third parties and is not used for advertising, analytics or tracking. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Permissions

  • Camera: scanning QR codes and evidence photos
  • Location: only to read the connected Wi-Fi network name; location is never stored or sent
  • Local network: connecting to the institution's server
  • Face ID / Touch ID: app lock (biometric data never leaves the device)

Deletion and your rights

You can remove your device from the web (Profile › Mobile devices). For deletion of your account and data, or other requests, contact your institution's Quality Unit or msoguz44@gmail.com.
